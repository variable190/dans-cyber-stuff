# Business Logic Vulnerabilities

## What are Business Logic Vulnerabilities?

Business Logic Vulnerabilities arise when an application fails to properly enforce its intended business rules and workflows.

## Exploits

- [Workflow Bypass](business-logic-vulnerabilities/workflow-bypass.md)
- [Price Manipulation](business-logic-vulnerabilities/price-manipulation.md)
- [Race Conditions](business-logic-vulnerabilities/race-conditions.md)

## Impact

Fraud, unauthorized actions.

## Prevention

Server-side rule enforcement, threat modeling.

## Tools

Burp, scripts.
